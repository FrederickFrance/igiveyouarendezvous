# Code of conduct

igiveyouarendezvous is a small personal project, and everyone is welcome
here, whatever their experience, their background or their language.

In the issues, the pull requests and everywhere else in the project:

- be kind and patient, in particular with those who are not developers:
  many users come from human resources, not from programming;
- discuss ideas and code, not people; a disagreement is fine, contempt is
  not;
- no harassment, insults, discriminatory remarks or jokes, sexual content,
  or personal information about others published without their consent.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close discussions, and block
people who do not respect these rules.

To report a problem privately, write to the maintainer on
[LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/frederic-sauvage-rueangpanichpiban).
Every report is read, and kept confidential.

These rules are inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
